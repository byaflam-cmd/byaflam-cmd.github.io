Booking policies
Tap to read
      
Please read these before you book. Sending a request means you agree to them.

      

        
Deposit
    
          
  • After we confirm your shoot, we'll send your invoice. A 25% deposit is due within 2 days to hold your date (or the day before your shoot, if that's sooner).
    • 
          
  • The rest (75%) is due day of shoot.
    • 
          
  • Your date isn't booked until we receive the deposit.
    • 
          
  • Deposits are non-refundable.
 
Where we shoot
     
  • We serve Houston and surrounding areas.
    •  
  • Shooting farther out? Ask us and we'll let you know.

        
Payment
    
          
  • Deposit: within 2 days of your invoice. The rest: the day of the shoot.
    • 
          
  • We accept Cash App ($byaflam) and Zelle ((832) 648-9386). Please put your name and shoot date in the note.
    • 
          
  • Photos and videos are delivered once the full balance is paid.

        
Cancelling or rescheduling
    
          
  • We need at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
    • 
          
  • With 24+ hours' notice, your deposit can move to a new date once.
    • 
          
  • Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice, or not showing up, means your deposit is forfeited.
    • 
          
  • If we ever have to cancel, you get your full deposit back.

        
Running late
    
          
  • Sessions start and end at the booked time. If you're late, we can't promise extra time, and the full price still applies.
    • 
          
  • Need more time on the day? Extra hours can be added if our schedule allows.

        
Liability
    
          
  • By booking, you agree Aflam isn't responsible for injury, illness, or loss or damage to personal property during your session.
    • 
          
  • We're not responsible for things outside our control, like weather, venue rules, or lab or equipment problems. If we can't deliver what you booked, the most we owe you is a refund of what you paid.

        
Your photos
    
          
  • We may share some photos and clips on our page and website. Tell us in your notes if you'd rather we didn't.
    • 
          
  • Edited photos are usually ready within 2–3 weeks. Film takes longer because it has to be developed.

      

      
Questions about any of this? Email byaflam@gmail.com or DM @byaflam before you book.
